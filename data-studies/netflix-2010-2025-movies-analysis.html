Netflix 2010-2025 Movies Analysis

May 20, 2026

This sample page tests the new Data Studies reading layout using the same narrow editorial width as the About Me page. The goal is to keep the analysis focused, readable, and self-contained without introducing card-heavy dashboard patterns.

For this mock study, the core question is simple: how did Netflix's movie catalog evolve between 2010 and 2025? A finished version would likely break this down by release volume, genre concentration, movie duration patterns, and the balance between older licensed titles and newer additions.

What This Page Is Testing

  • A narrow reading column that keeps all content within one controlled width.
  • A text-first structure where insight and interpretation come before visual complexity.
  • A reusable page shell for future public-dataset and niche-data studies.

When you review this page in the browser, the main thing to evaluate is whether the reading experience feels calm and intentional enough for longer-form analysis.

Sample Findings Direction

A complete study could surface patterns such as whether Netflix expanded certain genres more aggressively in specific years, whether movie runtimes clustered differently across periods, and whether catalog growth aligned with broader platform strategy changes.

Because this is a test page, the content is intentionally lightweight. What matters right now is validating the layout, navigation behavior, theme handling, and how comfortably future charts or tables can sit inside this narrower content format.
