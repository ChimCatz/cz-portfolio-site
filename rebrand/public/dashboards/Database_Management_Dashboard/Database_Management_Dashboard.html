Database Management Dashboard

The Database Management Dashboard provides a unified view of key metrics across Zoho CRM, a secondary CRM, data enrichment, VOIP 1, and VOIP 2 systems, covering lead and contact data quality, campaign performance, and overall CRM health. The dashboard is updated weekly and presents insights based on monthly metrics.

All figures shown are sample data for portfolio demonstration. Company, client, and vendor names have been anonymized. Built with Zoho Analytics.

Records by Country (Top 10)
Displays the top 10 countries with the highest number of records in the CRM.
Lead Category Funnel Stage
Displays counts of leads by category stage, from Uncategorized to Level 5 Converted. Values represent separate records, not a sequential funnel.
Contact Status
Shows the ratio between Active and Inactive Contacts in Zoho CRM
Key Lead Statuses
Shows key lead status metrics, highlighting the most important lead tags to track progress, engagement, and conversion performance.
Type of Lead
Shows the classification of each lead, such as Gamification, Bottom Funnel, Top Funnel, and other lead types based on campaign or stage.
Database Origin of Contacts
Displays the source of contacts, including data vendors, a secondary CRM, datamining, tech site registrations, and other lead generation channels.
Contacts by Job Level
Displays the distribution of contacts by job level, highlighting roles such as C-Level, Director, Manager, and Vice President to show where most records fall.
Email Verification Status
Word cloud showing the distribution of email verification statuses such as Valid, Unverified, and Bounced, highlighting the most common verification results.
