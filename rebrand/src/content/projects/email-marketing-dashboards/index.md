## Two questions

Email reporting comes down to two questions: how are the campaigns doing, and which mailing lists are worth sending to? Each dashboard answers one of them. Use the tabs above to switch between the two.

## Email Campaigns Overview

**This month against last.** Emails sent this month next to last month's figure, with leads, opens, clicks, and click rate beside it.

**Monthly trend.** One chart of emails sent, opens, clicks, and bounces by month.

**Funnel.** Emails sent, then the ones that didn't bounce, then opens, then clicks, with the percentage that survives each step.

**Campaign table.** Every campaign on one row: revenue, cost, emails sent, opens, open rate, clicks, and click rate. The table scrolls inside its tile.

## Lists Performance Dashboard

**Totals.** Contacts, campaigns sent, and unsubscribes across all lists, plus the list that generated the most leads.

**By list.** One chart compares campaigns sent, contacts, and unsubscribes for each mailing list. A second shows the leads each list generated from email and SMS campaigns.

**List table.** The same numbers for every list on one row, so a strong or weak list can be looked up by name.

## Why split it in two

A campaign and a mailing list are different things to judge. A campaign is judged on what happened after one send. A list is judged on what it keeps producing across many sends, and on how many people leave it. Keeping them on separate dashboards keeps each page focused on one decision.
